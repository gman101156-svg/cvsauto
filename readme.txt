Deployment:
1. Sign in to Netlify and open the existing cvs-auto site.
2. Deploy this folder (or its contents) as a new production deploy.
3. In Netlify, enable/confirm Forms and check the Forms tab for model-one-interest submissions.
4. Test the form with a real test submission after deployment.

Important: This is an early-interest/contact form, not an order, reservation, deposit, or purchase system.
CVS Model One remains a feasibility-stage concept. No price, delivery date, or inventory is promised.
